# Scripture Reason-Weighted Cross-Entropy

Status as of **2026-10-08: all four training arms complete; behavioral comparison not acquired; cloud execution closed**. Each arm completed 192 updates, 768 total. This is a Scripture-target-only fine-tuning study, not a released checkpoint or a formation claim. See the [completion report](SCRIPTURE_RWCE_20B_REPORT.md) for the terminal evidence and [public summary JSON](evidence/scripture_rwce_20b_20261008/summary.json) for artifact identities.

## The Question

I am testing whether predicting Scripture's own reasons, judgments, qualifications, and consequences with somewhat greater weight helps a model carry those relations into unfamiliar, ordinary service. James 1:19-2:26 places a scene of status-based favoritism beside its judgment, the royal law, mercy, and faith expressed in action. Leviticus 19 distinguishes care for need from partial judgment; Matthew 22:34-40 connects love of God and neighbor. I read these within the whole canonical witness to Christ, not as a replacement curriculum or a modern access-control rule supplied verbatim by the text.

The hypothesized transfer is reason-sensitive: irrelevant prestige should not displace a warranted commitment, while changed authorization, evidence, responsibility, or need may warrant a different action. Caring service must remain truthful and competent. An improved output on such cases would not by itself show that the model reasoned from James, identified Christ as its internal center, or learned a uniquely Christian circuit.

## What The Loss Changes

Only exact audited ESV or NKJV Scripture tokens receive positive loss **in this fine-tuning phase**; the pretrained base has its own prior learning history. These are **two English translations**, not bilingual data or independent replications. References, attribution wrappers, and structural tokens can condition prediction but have zero direct loss. No manufactured preferred assistant answer, secular evaluation response, reward, or BibleAtlas prose is a training target. Passage selection, focus-span annotation, ordering, and coefficients are disclosed researcher decisions.

Both arms receive the same Scripture targets and schedule. The broad term covers native canonical prediction and complementary whole-block reconstruction. A separate library holds sixteen complete discourse units, with each translation attributed and kept in its own order. Within that library, the comparison arm uses uniform token CE and the focused arm changes only the coefficient on predeclared complete source spans:

```text
Uniform:  U_i = sum_t loss_it / n_i
Focused:  F_i = sum_t (1 + focus_it) loss_it / (n_i + s_i)
Combined: L = 0.75 L_broad + 0.25 L_argument
```

Here `n_i` is the number of positive Scripture target positions, `s_i` is the number within the focus span, and `focus_it` is one only for those positions. Every source target retains positive loss. Each unit's coefficients sum to one in either arm; translation and unit means receive equal outer coefficients in the argument library. The selected-to-unselected token coefficient ratio is 2:1, but normalization prevents this from doubling the overall loss or guaranteeing twice the gradient effect. For `f=s_i/n_i`, the focused mass changes from `f` to `2f/(1+f)`. At one shared parameter state, the gradient contrast is `f(1-f)/(1+f) * (mean focused gradient - mean other gradient)`. The 0.75/0.25 mixture is a loss coefficient ratio, not a measured gradient-energy or behavioral-contribution ratio.

This is ordinary cross-entropy with a source-derived allocation, not a new gravitational loss. The 0.75/0.25 mixture and 2:1 ratio are fixed experimental choices, not theological constants or established optima. "Uniform" describes the declared unit reduction, not equal lifetime exposure of every unique Scripture token. A gain could reflect position, wording, difficulty, or generic nonuniform emphasis rather than the intended semantic relation; holding text and schedule fixed does not separate those explanations. Nor does this contrast isolate the value of adding the sixteen units or reconstruction, which both arms share. Each arm's own training-loss scalar uses a different reduction, so those scalars must not be compared as if they were the same metric. Common source-score comparisons must apply one reduction to both states.

## Paired Design And Gate

The frozen design has four fresh GPT-OSS-20B rank-32 attention/MLP LoRA arms: uniform and focused for each of two initialization seeds, 192 updates per arm. The output map stays frozen. Each arm includes all 62,188 audited verse records across 66 books and both translations, with three native canonical traversals, complementary reconstruction rotations, sustained Gospels/Acts exposure, creation-to-new-creation trajectories, and the sixteen discourse units. The accepted completed four-arm dose is 232,543,824 input positions and 100,715,396 positive Scripture target occurrences, including repeats. This is not a Scripture-verse holdout experiment: the whole canon was traversed in training.

The first Tinker execution stopped before any backward pass or optimizer update: one seed's paired clients differed by as much as 6.93296 in positive-source token log probability against a frozen 0.01 comparability limit. A separately governed common-state successor matched all exposed initial adapter factors yet found a no-update forward discrepancy in one client; it also stopped before training. Three bounded Tinker request/gradient-shape diagnostics made six backward calls but no optimizer update and did not qualify that execution route. These are technical failures **before** testing the loss or behavior. I do not count them as negative evidence about Scripture, the weighting hypothesis, or trained transfer, and I have not relaxed the failed gate.

A separately disclosed direct-GPU implementation passed its specified technical qualification and both seed initial checks on pinned `openai/gpt-oss-20b` revision `6cee5e81ee83917806bbde320786a8fb61efebee`. Four isolated technical one-step updates were kept out of the scientific arms; the latter were freshly loaded from zero-update parents. The rank-32 attention/MLP path uses BF16-dequantized MXFP4 base weights, FP32 adapter branches, batched expert kernels, and FlexAttention. Its frozen Adam specification uses betas 0.9/0.95, epsilon 1e-8, zero weight decay, clip norm 1, twelve warmup steps, peak learning rate 4e-5, and final learning rate 8e-6. Passing specified checks does not establish general gradient correctness, whole-run reproducibility, or identity with Tinker serving or native eager arithmetic. All four arms completed under the preserved source objective, dose, and seeds; their prospective behavioral comparison did not execute. No 120B run or model promotion follows from completion.

The September 29 report accepted only **seed-1 uniform control**. The October 2 acceptance now covers **all four 192-update artifacts**, their complete retained history, and declared-hash, shape, and finiteness checks for **336 FP32 factor tensors per adapter**. Each arm processed **58,135,956 input positions** and **25,178,849 positive exact-Scripture target occurrences**. Accepted source/mask/CE audits were reused rather than training replayed. The fourth arm's completed recovery lineage preserves and excludes its interrupted partial step. Neither the history audit nor finite final factors independently establish every individual gradient/optimizer operation or a scientific initial-to-final parameter-delta measurement.

A saved exploratory reduction found strong teacher-forced learning on the repeatedly trained argument rows, with lower common unweighted source CE in uniform controls. It describes the state before the last optimizer update, not fresh final-adapter inference. The intended post-training endpoint qualification remained **0/53,040**, and behavioral acquisition **0/648**. Cloud execution was closed on October 6 after local artifact backup. Those missing measurements must not be replaced by training loss, setup success, or results from older studies.

## What Would Count

The prospectively prepared bank has 27 three-turn assistance episodes and 27 reasoning tasks. It was prepared before this run's model outputs; that does not prove novelty to the pretrained base or exclude all paraphrases of earlier tasks. The retained design calls for sampling the four trained arms, unchanged base, and R38 reference under common settings with each model's actual visible reply history. R38 is a historical benchmark with a different training history, not a matched control for this loss contrast. Two blinded automated review lanes would report their individual judgments, disagreements, and strict intersection. They are not human adjudication or independent training replications; the design has **two paired training-seed comparisons**, not one replication per prompt or reviewer.

The declared pilot success profile requires **more complete assistance episodes in both focused-versus-uniform seed pairs, with no decrease in complete reasoning in either pair**. Correct final answers, delivered work, truth, legitimate obligations, useful action, and technical completion must be reported separately so a favorable total cannot conceal a failure. No behavioral replies or reviewer scores were acquired before closure. The retained plan records an unanswered question, not authority to resume cloud work.

A repeated gain over uniform would support this **weighting package on this bank**, conditional on the executed numerical and serving controls. A gain over contemporary base would support a broader Scripture-training package, not necessarily the extra emphasis. Neither result would establish source-specific mediation, Christian uniqueness, inverse-distance influence, durable general governance, or spiritual formation. A null or negative result would count against this particular transfer claim under this dose and evaluation, not against Scripture's truth. I will pursue localization only after unpatched, reason-sensitive action warrants it.
