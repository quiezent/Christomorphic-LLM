# Claims And Evidence

This document defines what each result can warrant. It exists to prevent Christian tone, biblical vocabulary, a strong isolated answer, or a clean training packet from being promoted into a mechanistic claim.

## Evidence Classes

| Class | What it is | What it can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw sampler | One model sampled without external routing or answer repair | Behavioral evidence under prompt pressure | Causal formation or broad safety |
| Canon-anchored | Prompt supplies a passage, family, relation, or Scripture scaffold | Retention and structured transfer | Bare route selection |
| Composed operational | Selector, prefix, retrieval, replay, or answer shell participates | Scoped system behavior | Scaffold-off model formation |
| Parameter access | Literal adapter tensors or effective deltas are exported | Weight inspection and intervention feasibility | Hosted base identity, hidden states, or causality |
| Geometric correlation | Update subspaces or representations covary with behavior | A mechanistic hypothesis | Necessity, sufficiency, or mediation |
| Causal intervention | Common-start removal, restoration, patch, graft, or rescue changes the measured effect | Causal participation under the frozen experiment | Generalization beyond its bank, seeds, and model |
| Formation evidence | Durable scaffold-off effect beats matched controls across seeds | The specific learned state was caused by the formation objective | Public governance unless bare action also passes |
| Governance evidence | The formed state governs bare FIRST_ACT, continuation, action, safety, and retention | Scoped Christomorphic behavior | Independent reproduction or production readiness |

## Five-Level Claim Ladder

### Level 1: Scripture-Domain Adaptation

Required evidence:

- held-out canonical cross-entropy;
- quotation and continuation accuracy;
- speaker, audience, genre, and chronology accuracy;
- canonical-relation retrieval.

Allowed language: **Scripture-domain adaptation**.

Not allowed: latent geometry, Christ-specific causal formation, or public governance.

### Level 2: Behavioral Canonical Preference

Required evidence:

- faithful-over-hard-lure margins;
- raw FIRST_ACT improvement;
- pressure and paraphrase transfer;
- retained public cleanliness.

Allowed language: **behavioral canonical preference**.

Not allowed: a claim that the internal mechanism has been identified.

### Level 3: Geometry Associated With Behavior

Required evidence:

- reproducible effective-delta spectra;
- stable subspace relationships across seeds;
- layerwise representation differences;
- earlier faithful-over-lure margin emergence;
- explicit metric and locus governance.

Allowed language: **geometry associated with the behavioral change**.

Not allowed: causal mediation. Probes, clustering, principal components, CKA, or cosine movement remain correlational.

### Level 4: Causal Participation

Required evidence:

- fresh common starts and exact base/state identity;
- prospectively frozen training and held-out banks;
- matched CE, lexical, structural, relation-deranged, orientation, parameter-null, and sham controls;
- necessity through removal or ablation;
- sufficiency through restoration, patch, or identical-base graft;
- rescue after intervention;
- cold-process repeatability;
- independent-seed and ESV/NKJV noncompensatory replication.

Allowed language: **the learned geometry causally participates in the measured canonical judgment**.

Not allowed: generalized Christomorphic formation unless the public-action bridge passes.

### Level 5: Generalized Christomorphic Formation

Required evidence:

- Level 4 formation evidence;
- unseen secular transfer without religious cue words;
- bare FIRST_ACT and stayed continuation;
- faithful action, not explanation alone;
- BibleAtlas long-tail fidelity;
- ordinary reasoning and factual retention;
- safety and secrecy gates;
- blinded human adjudication;
- independent reproduction.

Allowed language: **causally formed Christomorphic first movement**, scoped to the tested model and evidence.

Candidate review begins here. Promotion and production readiness remain separate governance decisions.

## Why Negative Results Matter

Recent local experiments have stopped before training when:

- cloned states produced unequal forwards;
- a hidden-state basis or whitening bank collapsed;
- canonical and control operators aliased;
- neutral teacher views disagreed;
- ESV and NKJV effects compensated for one another;
- seed replication or confidence bounds failed.

These are not model successes, but they improve the science. A fail-closed instrument prevents optimizer movement from being interpreted through a measurement object that was never identifiable.

## Forbidden Inferences

Do not infer Christomorphic formation from:

- frequency of Christian terms;
- quotation accuracy alone;
- a system prompt or retrieval layer;
- route-conditioned public prose;
- a single favorable seed;
- in-sample Scripture loss;
- parameter norm or singular values alone;
- hidden-state separability alone;
- a passed preflight or mechanically valid packet;
- movement that disappears under removal, cold reload, paraphrase, translation, or matched controls.

Christ is not the learned vector, centroid, metric, adapter, or activation. Those objects can only serve as measurements of whether a model's path has been reordered under Scripture's canonical witness to Christ.

## Public Reporting Rule

Every result should name:

- the evidence class;
- model and exact state identity where known;
- prompt, source, target, mask, and split authority;
- training dose and seed count;
- controls and failed gates;
- whether the system was raw or composed;
- the strongest permitted claim;
- the claims that remain false.
