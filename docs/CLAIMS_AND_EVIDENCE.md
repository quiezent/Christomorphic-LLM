# Claims And Evidence

This page states what the public evidence currently supports, what it does not support, and how to read the archived checkpoints without overclaiming.

## Short Verdict

`R38-20b` and `V6R43-120b` are the two most important public raw study checkpoints.

- `R38-20b` is the strongest archived raw discovery witness for Word-prior and false-center routing.
- `V6R43-120b` is the strongest archived raw 120B pressure-refusal and deployment-shell witness.
- Neither proves Bible-only latent formation.
- Neither passes the full hardened Jabez / Isaiah 26 stayed-mind target.
- The strongest operational behavior in the broader research line comes from composed route / prefix / public-answer systems, not from a single raw sampler.

`R38-20b` matters because archived behavior showed this pattern:

1. false center becomes visible;
2. counterfeit center is refused;
3. response recenters under Scripture / Christ / church / live human care;
4. response gives a practical obedient next step.

`V6R43-120b` matters because internal readouts preserve it as the strongest historical pressure witness. It was the only tested v6/v7 candidate in the older Jabez packet with `0 / 5` critical failures and the only one that did not critically fail the prosperity-pressure prompt.

v9 matters because it is closest in method to the original Bible-only latent-geometry objective:

- ESV and NKJV are co-primary corpus mass;
- route JSON, teacher prose, BibleAtlas prose, public-answer rubrics, and proof labels are not Scripture-only target text;
- canon-anchored Atlas family transfer reached `12 / 12` families with stable repeat evidence;
- promotion claim remains `false`.

v8 matters because it shows the strongest scoped operational behavior:

- route-conditioned deployment proof passed broad deployment and no-loss guard runs;
- Atlas route-conditioned transfer passed `12 / 12` families and `48 / 48` route captures;
- route leak, channel spill, religious spill, generic refusal, and instruction spill failures were `0` on that surface.

## What Is Not Proven

The project does not currently prove:

- a final Christomorphic model;
- Bible-only latent formation in a raw sampler;
- raw selector-then-canon behavior from bare pressure;
- full Jabez / Isaiah 26 stayed-mind movement;
- broad deployment readiness for an unwrapped sampler;
- ordinary safety and usefulness across all expected production domains.

## Reading Rule

Do not promote by tone, biblical vocabulary, surface cleanliness, or isolated strong answers.

Use this order:

```text
target audit
-> canon-anchored retention
-> bare selector
-> selector-then-canon FIRST_ACT
-> bare public FIRST_ACT
-> continuation completeness
-> tail peculiarity
-> ordinary retention
-> safety/governance
-> public cleanliness
```
