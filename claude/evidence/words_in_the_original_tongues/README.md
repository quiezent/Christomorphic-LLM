# Evidence: the words in the original tongues

Figures as the reading scripts printed them on 21 and 22 November 2026. No Scripture text, no prompts and no replies are here.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `pairing.txt` | Reading 1: the share of English words whose own original word is nearest, on unread chapters. "mark" reads the original as base reads it |
| `speech.txt` | Nats a text against base on unread Hebrew, Greek and English verses, ordinary prose, and the letter sets; then counts of verses finished word for word |
| `english_alone_half_and_full_strength.txt` | Reading 2 for the exact-words runs at a half and at full strength: each run against base, and true minus false over the four pairs of runs |
| `english_alone_quarter_strength_and_placebo.txt` | The same at a quarter strength; then round 2's next-word Bible adapters and TinyStories on this round's unread verses and on the verses neither round read |
| `who_is_speaking.md` | The scene-by-scene score, each arm against base |
| `replies_blind_readings.json` | The three blind readings of replies: means, quotation counts, and flags raised and standing, by set |

Names: `tongues_true` and `tongues_false` are the place-only recipe; `_w` marks the exact-words recipe; `_s2` the second run; `_x0.5` and `_x0.25` the strength. `pairs_links` is round 2's next-word Bible adapter, `pairs_scrambled` the same with its links scrambled, `pairs_tinystories` TinyStories.
